Welcome to the new home for our stories. This is where the **Mouseketeers** —
and the young people, partners and communities they work with — share what's
happening inside Always In The Club Foundation.

Expect a mix of:

- behind-the-scenes notes from our campaigns and events,
- spotlights on mentees, mentors and partners,
- and the occasional reflection on why we do what we do.

> Once in the Club… **Always In The Club.**

## Coming up

In the weeks ahead we'll be publishing recaps of recent campaigns —
*#MMC35 @ 90s Con*, *Day of Hope*, and the latest from *Disney Imagination
Campus* — plus interviews with the Mouseketeers leading our mentoring and
arts education work.

If there's something you'd love to read about, [drop us a line](mailto:Info@AlwaysInTheClub.org).
We read every note.

Thank you for being here.
