#### Understanding Your Creative Control Room

In Inside Out, Riley's emotions worked together in Headquarters to guide her actions. Similarly, our mentorship program helps artists understand how different emotional states influence their creative process:

• Joy: The passion that drives artistic excellence

• Sadness: The depth that enriches performance authenticity

• Fear: The precision that refines technique

• Anger: The determination that overcomes obstacles

• Disgust: The discernment that shapes artistic choices
