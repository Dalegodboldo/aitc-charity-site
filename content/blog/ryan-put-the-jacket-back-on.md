If you were anywhere near your phone last Friday night, you saw it.

Ryan Gosling walked onto the Disney Entertainment Showcase stage at D23 in front of 14,000 people wearing a retro Mickey Mouse Club jacket. Director Shawn Levy had just played a clip of him as a teenager on the show. Then Ryan came out dressed like it was 1993.

> "I owe a lot to Disney. Had they not looked far and wide and come to my hometown, I would not be here today. And now here I am 30 years later, I'm back and it's changing my life again."

## Why that line landed the way it did

Disney did look far and wide. Between 1989 and 1996 the All-New Mickey Mouse Club held open calls across the country, pulled kids out of gymnasiums and community theaters, and put them into a working television production in Orlando with acting coaches, vocal coaches, choreographers, tutors, and a shooting schedule that would wear out most adults.

Some of those kids became household names. Most of them did not, and their experience inside that building is just as much a part of what happened there.

That is the part almost nobody has told. Until now.

## The full story is on the record

*Always In The Club: The True Story of the All-New Mickey Mouse Club* is built from interviews with more than 60 members of the cast, crew, and former executives. Three volumes covering how Disney found them, how the training actually worked, what a day on that soundstage looked like, and what happened after the cameras stopped.

It also covers something people do not expect from a kids' variety show in 1991: the adults on that production paid attention to these kids as people, not just performers. The education. The discipline. The support when the schedule got heavy. The question of who these kids were going to be after the show ended.

That is the part that eventually became a foundation.

## Which brings us to now

Cast members who came up through that system reunited and built Always In The Club Foundation around a straightforward idea. What changed their lives was the commitment the adults made to them.
