## **A Dream Long in the Making: Celebrate Independence Day With the Launch of Our Mickey & Friends Collection!**

We are thrilled to announce that our [**Mickey & Friends Collection**](https://mmcreunion.com/collections/mickey-and-friends-collection) featuring the leader of the Club himself is finally here—and it’s been a long time coming!

As we celebrate the Fourth of July and all the spirit of independence, community, and creativity that this day represents, we couldn’t imagine a better moment to share this special launch with you. The Mickey & Friends Collection is more than just a set of officially licensed designs—it’s a heartfelt tribute to the legacy of the “All New” Mickey Mouse Club, the enduring magic of Disney’s most beloved characters, and the power of mentorship and the arts to change lives.

### **Why This Collection Matters**

For years, our community has asked for something that brings together the nostalgia of the Club, the inspiration of Disney, and a way to support the next generation of dreamers. This collection is our answer—a celebration of friendship, creativity, and the joy that comes from being “Always In The Club.”

Curated by reunited Mouseketeers, the collection features officially licensed pieces, many with a playful, throwback vibe—perfect for sparking memories, inspiring smiles, and connecting generations. Whether you’re looking for a unique gift or a keepsake for yourself, these designs capture the fun and heart of classic Disney.

And the best part? **100% of the profits from your purchase benefit youth mentoring and arts education through Always In The Club Foundation.** When you shop, you’re not just adding a unique piece of Disney to your collection—you’re investing in the future of young creatives.

### **What’s Next—And Why Now**

This launch is just the beginning. The Mickey & Friends Collection is the first in our brand-new [**Disney Design Collection**](https://mmcreunion.com/collections/disney-design-collection), which will expand later this year to include even more of your favorites—think Pixar, Star Wars, Marvel, and classic Disney characters. We’re building something special, and you’re at the heart of it.

![](/images/blog/untitled-design-94--99352031.png)

We know it’s been a long journey to get here. From brainstorming sessions with Mouseketeers to carefully curating every piece, this project has been fueled by passion, persistence, and the support of fans like you. Today, we get to share the results—and the excitement—with our entire community.

### **Celebrate With Us!**

As you enjoy the festivities of Independence Day, we invite you to stop by our [**Club Store**](https://mmcreunion.com/collections/mickey-and-friends-collection) and experience the Mickey & Friends Collection for yourself. Whether you’re a lifelong fan of Disney or the Mouse Club, a supporter of youth mentorship, or simply someone who loves great design, there’s something here for you.

Thank you for believing in our mission, for keeping the magic alive, and for making this dream a reality. We can’t wait to see how you style your new favorites—and how, together, we’ll inspire the next generation.
