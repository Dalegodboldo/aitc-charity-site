Imagine a place where learning comes alive, where education transcends traditional classroom walls, and where young minds are inspired by the magic of Disney. Welcome to "Destination: Disney Imagination Campus," a transformative program brought to you by the Always In The Club Foundation.

Led by former Mouseketeers who once called Disney their creative home, this unique educational experience offers students an extraordinary opportunity to develop their skills in performing arts and academic subjects at the most magical places on Earth - Disneyland® and Walt Disney World® Resort.

![](/images/blog/disney-campus-bright-537c76f0.png)

Why Disney Imagination Campus?

For over 60 years, Disney has been at the forefront of educational innovation, creating workshops that empower young people to grow, learn, and thrive. Our program combines this rich educational heritage with the mentorship of experienced industry professionals, including original Mouseketeers who understand the transformative power of Disney's creative environment.

What Students Experience:

Immersive workshops in performing arts and academicsHands-on learning with Disney professionalsLeadership development opportunitiesBroadway-style performance workshopsPhysics and science programs in a real-world setting

Making Dreams Accessible:

The Always In The Club Foundation is on a mission to sponsor 1,000 children to attend Disney Imagination Campus. Through our scholarship program, we're making this transformative experience accessible to students from all backgrounds, ensuring that financial circumstances don't stand in the way of educational opportunity.
