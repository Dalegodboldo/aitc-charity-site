The ocean calls, just as creativity beckons. Like Moana's journey beyond the reef of Motunui, every young artist faces that pivotal moment when they must decide to venture beyond their comfort zone. The coaches and mentors at Always In The Club Foundation have spent 25+ years helping young creatives answer their calling.

![Copyright: Disney](/images/blog/moana-68c83870.webp)Copyright: Disney

**When the Heart of Te Fiti Calls** Just as Moana was chosen by the ocean, we believe every young creative has a unique gift waiting to be discovered. Our "Destination: Disney Imagination Campus" program isn't just accessing workshops by Disney – it's a voyage of discovery. We've watched young artists find their "Te Fiti" – that inner spark that makes them shine.

**Your Maui Moments** Remember how Moana needed Maui's guidance to become a wayfinder? Our mentorship program pairs young creatives with industry veterans who've navigated these waters for decades. These mentors, like our own Mouseketeers and entertainment professionals, share more than just techniques – they share wisdom, experience, and the kind of insights that can only come from years of "wayfinding" in the entertainment industry.

**Know Who You Are** "I am Moana of Motunui. You will board my boat, sail across the sea, and restore the heart of Te Fiti." Just as Moana found strength in knowing her identity, our programs help young artists develop their creative identity. Through hands-on training and a supportive community, our teachers have helped over 3,500 young people globally find their voice and purpose.

Ready to begin your own creative voyage? Your journey starts here. Visit [**www.Learn-Grow-Thrive.org**](http://www.learn-grow-thrive.org/) to book a coach or apply for mentorship with industry professionals who can help guide your path in entertainment. Like Moana, your story of discovery awaits – take the first step today.
